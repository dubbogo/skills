# Code of Conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_CN.md) | English

Respect different languages, backgrounds and experience levels. Critique behavior and evidence. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information, impersonation and repeated disruption are not.

This applies to repository Issues, PRs, reviews and project-maintained community spaces, including maintainers' behavior.

Report public concerns to repository maintainers through an Issue when safe. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact without publishing identities or incident details. No dedicated private conduct mailbox is configured in this version; do not guess an address. See [Support](SUPPORT.md).

Maintainers may seek clarification, request correction, hide harmful content, limit participation or use platform moderation for serious or repeated violations. Consider context, explain actions when safe and avoid retaliation. An involved person should not be the sole decision maker; seek another maintainer's review when possible.
